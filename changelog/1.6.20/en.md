- macOS: the launcher starts the native Mac version of Dawn of War II: Retribution (Feral, from the Mac Steam) as vanilla with the crossplay patch (crossplay_fix), so a Mac player plays with Windows players. Vanilla is the only game on a Mac: Elite's Windows add-ons do not load in the native game.
- macOS: finds Steam (Steam.app or its folder) and the game in every Steam library (the default one and the ones on other disks from libraryfolders.vdf); Settings → Game folder says "Steam: found · DoW2 (Feral 1.0.3): found at …", Browse takes the game folder, its .app or a Steam library.
- macOS: the crossplay patch is installed and updated before Play (up to 1.6.19 it was never installed for vanilla); Play starts Steam first when it is not running, then the game; the game's output goes into Send logs (mac_game.log) with what was found of Steam and the game.

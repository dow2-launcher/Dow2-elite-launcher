- When the launcher starts it checks the way to Relic's game servers (their ports, the delay and a stream of ~110 KB). If it has trouble - closed ports, a delay over 200 ms, or a stream that stops (common in Russia and Ukraine) - or if you used "every game through our server" before 1.4.0 - the launcher offers our forwarding servers with one button. Nothing is turned on by itself; "Not now" hides the offer for the month.

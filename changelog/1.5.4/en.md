- Version switch and update: the bar tells what is being done to the file right now (checking, from the local cache, reading the old file, downloading, building, verifying), how many MB of that file are done, which file of how many, the speed and about how long is left. Downloaded bytes show at once, not after the whole file.
- Faster version switch: files of the previous version are not hashed again (the launcher knows them from its own list), files from the local cache and the replaced files are moved instead of copied (hundreds of MB less written), a big file is downloaded in 16 MB parts - a dropped connection costs at most one part.
- Versions our add-ons do not work with (e.g. grantovich's IG Rework): while such a version is installed every add-on is off and out of the game, the ticks are greyed out with a note. Back on the main version your choices return.

- New tab **Replays**. *My replays*: the replays of the game on this computer with the map, the players, their races, heroes and army levels, the length, the date and the game version (3.2.1, 3.1, vanilla...), with search by mode, number of players, races and version. One button puts a replay into the shared pool; you are its author there and can write a description.
- *Shared pool*: the replays of all players - the ladder matches, the ones sent from the launcher and from the community Discord - with the same search, the author, the description and the server's analysis of the match. Download puts the file into the game's replays (Replays in the main menu).
